What the stats and abilities mean
  • BS / WS: the dice roll needed to hit, before modifiers.
  • Strength and Toughness: together, these determine the wound roll.
  • AP: makes armour saves harder. Invulnerable saves ignore AP.
  • Damage: wounds removed by each unsaved attack.
  • Sustained Hits: extra hits on a critical hit.
  • Lethal Hits: critical hits wound automatically.
  • Devastating Wounds: critical wounds bypass saves.
  • Torrent: hits automatically.
  • Twin-linked: reroll failed wound rolls.
  • Blast: one extra attack for every five starting target models.
  • Rapid Fire: extra attacks within half range.
  • Feel No Pain: a roll to ignore each wound lost.

Presets fill in supported weapon abilities for you.

Use the checkboxes for rules that depend on range or another condition. This calculator doesn’t handle Precision, Hazardous self-damage, movement or legal target checks. Rules left out are listed beside the unit and saved with the result.

How to read these results

Average damage and kills show what you would expect over many rolls. You’ll get the same result each time for the same inputs: the calculator works out the probabilities directly. The typical damage range runs from the 10th to the 90th percentile, so it shows dice variation rather than an error margin.

Weapons attack in the order shown, and target groups take damage from top to bottom. The result covers that attack sequence. It doesn’t predict a whole game. Enter any cover bonus yourself; the calculator won’t check whether cover applies.

For a custom Anti weapon, set the critical wound roll after checking the target’s keywords. For Twin-linked, choose failed wound rerolls. Check the rules for your edition when using damage spill.
