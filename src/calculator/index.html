How to read these results

This calculates the probability of each possible damage outcome using your profiles and allocation order. Expected values are weighted averages of those outcomes. There is no random sampling or trial count; results are deterministic, subject to normal numerical rounding. Outcome ranges describe dice variability, not uncertainty in the calculation.

Weapon profiles resolve in the order shown. This models a single attack sequence against your ordered defensive groups, not movement, positioning or a full game. Save bonus is explicit; cover eligibility is not inferred. Hazardous self-damage, precision allocation and other unlisted rules are not modeled.

Anti-X can be represented by changing the critical wound threshold once you have checked the target keyword. Twin-linked can be represented by rerolling failed wound rolls. Check edition-specific damage interactions before using an ability.
